Ubud: Private Tour of Temples, Waterfalls & Rice Terraces

Embark on an enchanting journey through Bali with a private half-day or full-day tour guided by an expert local. Begin your adventure with a convenient pickup from your accommodation, setting the stage for a day of exploration. First, visit the Ubud Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary, where you’ll encounter the charming long-tailed monkeys in a lush, natural environment that feels both magical and alive. Next, wander through the breathtaking Tegallalang Rice Terrace, famed for its stunning vistas and traditional farming practices. Engage with local farmers to gain insights into the art of rice cultivation, deepening your appreciation for Balinese agriculture. Then, escape to the serene Ulu Petanu Waterfall nestled in Kedisan Village. This hidden gem offers a peaceful retreat, perfect for a refreshing swim amid the vibrant jungle surroundings. For lunch, savor a delicious meal at a restaurant with sweeping views of the rice terraces, an optional indulgence that enhances your experience. Afterward, visit Pura Tirta Empul Holy Water Temple, where the tranquil ambiance invites reflection and exploration of Bali's rich spiritual history. Conclude your adventure at a local coffee and tea plantation, where you’ll discover the intricate processes behind these beloved beverages. Participate in brewing techniques and sample unique blends that awaken your senses. Finally, enjoy a scenic drive back to your hotel, reflecting on the unforgettable experiences and the captivating beauty of Bali.
